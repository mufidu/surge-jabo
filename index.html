Data coverage & review

Topology symbols

Small amber routers at the bottom left show the Capacity value (IOH B1 or IOH POC). Dotted gray connectors indicate this capacity association, not FO progress. Blank Capacity cells have no marker. Town Launch badges show the original P1, P2, P3, or P1P2Ext. value; blank remarks have no badge. Click a cross-ring CSG reference or ring heading to open that ring. Site names appear above the IDs. Site stack: Site ID Surge → Site ID → RAN → site readiness → CSG router → Seq. CSG “NY” / “CSG NY” is displayed as CSG NY Installed (not yet installed). NY RFI means not yet ready for installation; RFI means ready for installation. CSG: Cell Site Gateway. FO: Fiber Optic. ACC: Access Router, per your reference. Router color shows installation/integration progress, not live health.

Reading this topology

Rings are arranged by sequence. Connections follow the Excel Segment field, with each ring in its own lane. Links to other rings end at a labeled CSG reference showing the destination site and ring; repeated CSG symbols represent the same site, not additional sites. Station endpoints represent ACC / Access Routers.

Huawei integration candidates

Only Huawei sites with Site Status RFI or Installed and Suggestion other than Freeze are included in this menu, its totals, filters and CSV export. NY RFI, Freeze, and already integrated sites are excluded. Path analysis uses the complete network, including upstream sites of other vendors.

Network-ready candidate: RFI / Installed sites qualify through completed FO (“6. Pulling done”, “7. Termination done”, or “8. RFS”) under these rules:

  1. Direct connection to a Station or Closure.
  2. Direct connection to a site that is On-Air / Integrated, or has CSG Integrated.
  3. Direct connection to a candidate qualifying under point 1 or 2.

Point 3 adds one candidate hop only; it does not recursively qualify further sites. Supporting sites may be from any vendor. NY RFI and Freeze sites cannot serve as point 1–2 candidates. Each assessment shows its qualifying point and supporting path.

Ready to schedule: a network-ready candidate whose site is Installed and whose CSG is Installed or Integrated. Other network-ready candidates show the equipment work still needed. This snapshot does not confirm optical tests or commissioning.

Required columns and rules

Ring, Seq (or Sequence), RAN, Site ID, Site ID Surge, Site Name, Suggestion, Site Status, FO Progress, Segment, FO Owner (or FO Vendor), CSG Integration Status (or CSG Remark). Optional City is used for regional filters. Optional Capacity is displayed as a small IOH marker.

Use unique Site IDs and unique sequence numbers within each ring. Endpoints without a site record are shown as unverified; Station names are ACC roots; Closure names are accepted for candidate analysis (including the source spelling “Closue”). Separate endpoints with semicolons. Suggestion must be Continue or Freeze. Save identifiers as text to preserve leading zeros. Recalculate and save formulas in Excel before importing.
